## Overview

AI ethics interviews probe your understanding of bias, fairness, alignment, and responsible deployment. Strong answers demonstrate awareness of real-world harms, regulatory landscape (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF), and practical mitigation strategies — not just philosophical positions.

---

## Core Concepts

### Algorithmic Bias
- **Definition:** Systematic, repeatable errors in an AI system that create unfair outcomes for specific groups.
- **Interview relevance:** Bias detection and mitigation is table-stakes knowledge for senior AI roles.
- **Common misconception:** "Bias only comes from biased training data" — model architecture, evaluation metrics, and deployment context all introduce bias.

### AI Alignment
- **Definition:** Ensuring AI systems pursue goals that reflect human intentions and values.
- **Interview relevance:** Demonstrates awareness of long-term AI safety beyond short-term performance.
- **Common misconception:** "Alignment is only relevant to AGI" — misaligned objectives cause real harm in production ML systems today.
